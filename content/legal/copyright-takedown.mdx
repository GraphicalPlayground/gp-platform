# Copyright Takedown Policy

Effective Date: July 26, 2026

Graphical Playground ("**GP**" or "**Graphical Playground**") respects the intellectual property
rights of others and expects users of its websites and the GP Platform (together, the
"**Services**") to do the same. This Copyright Takedown Policy (this "**Policy**") describes how
to report alleged copyright infringement in User Content, as defined in the
[Content Moderation Policy](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/content-moderation-policy),
and how GP responds to such reports. Reports of copyright infringement are handled under this
Policy rather than under Section 2 of the Content Moderation Policy. Reports concerning GP's
trademarks are handled under GP's Trademark Guidelines, available at
[graphical-playground.com/brand-guidelines](https://graphical-playground.com/brand-guidelines),
rather than under this Policy.

GP provides this Policy in accordance with Article 6-I-2 to 6-I-5 of French Law No. 2004-575 of
21 June 2004 for confidence in the digital economy (the "**LCEN**") and, because GP's
infrastructure is in part hosted in the United States, in accordance with the notice-and-takedown
procedure of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act, 17 U.S.C. § 512 (the "**DMCA**").

## 1. Filing a Copyright Infringement Notice

1.1. If you believe that material available on the Services infringes your copyright, send a
written notice to GP's designated agent at
[copyright@graphical-playground.com](mailto:copyright@graphical-playground.com) that includes:

(a) an electronic or physical signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on
their behalf;

(b) identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed;

(c) identification of the material you claim is infringing, including the specific URL or
location on the Services where it can be found;

(d) your name, postal address, telephone number, and email address;

(e) a statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the material in the manner
complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law; and

(f) a statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate
and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the copyright owner's behalf.

1.2. GP may disregard a notice that does not substantially comply with Section 1.1.

## 2. GP's Response

2.1. Upon receiving a notice that substantially complies with Section 1.1, GP will expeditiously
remove or disable access to the identified material and will notify the user who posted it that
the material has been removed and why.

2.2. If the identified material is hosted in a public repository under
[github.com/GraphicalPlayground](https://github.com/GraphicalPlayground), GP may direct you to
submit your notice through GitHub's own takedown process for that repository in addition to, or
instead of, this Policy.

## 3. Counter-Notification

3.1. If you believe material you posted was removed in error or misidentification, you may submit
a counter-notice to [copyright@graphical-playground.com](mailto:copyright@graphical-playground.com)
that includes:

(a) your physical or electronic signature;

(b) identification of the material removed and its location before removal;

(c) a statement, made under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was
removed as a result of mistake or misidentification;

(d) your name, postal address, and telephone number; and

(e) a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the competent courts of Lyon, France, or,
where the original complainant is located in the United States, the federal district court for
the judicial district in which your address is located, and that you will accept service of
process from the person who filed the original notice.

3.2. GP will forward a valid counter-notice to the person who submitted the original infringement
notice. If GP does not receive notice that the complainant has filed a legal action seeking to
restrain you from the allegedly infringing activity within ten (10) to fourteen (14) business
days of forwarding the counter-notice, GP may restore the removed material.

## 4. Repeat Infringers

4.1. Consistent with Section 5 of the Content Moderation Policy, GP will terminate, in
appropriate circumstances, the account of a user who is the subject of repeated, valid
infringement notices.

## 5. Misuse of This Policy

5.1. Submitting a knowingly false or materially misleading notice or counter-notice under this
Policy may expose you to liability, including under Article 6-I-4 of the LCEN, which provides for
criminal penalties of up to one year of imprisonment and a fine of €15,000 for using this
mechanism to obtain the removal of content the person knows to be lawful, and under 17 U.S.C. §
512(f) for material misrepresentations made under the DMCA.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

6.1. GP may update this Policy from time to time. GP will post the most current version at
[graphical-playground.com/legal/copyright-takedown](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/copyright-takedown).
