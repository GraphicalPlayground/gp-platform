# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy (this "**Policy**") describes how Graphical Playground ("**GP**" or
"**Graphical Playground**") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal data when you visit
GP's websites or use the GP Platform (together, the "**Services**"), as defined in the
[Terms of Service](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/terms-of-service). This Policy applies
to visitors, learners, Authorized Users, and any other individual whose personal data GP
processes in connection with the Services ("**you**"). GP's corporate identity and registration
details are set out in the
[Legal Notice](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/legal-notice). Where you access the
Services on behalf of an institutional Customer under a
[Data Processing Addendum](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/data-processing), that
addendum, rather than this Policy, governs GP's processing of personal data as a processor on the
Customer's behalf.

## 1. Data Controller

1.1. For the purposes of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the "**GDPR**") and the French Loi n° 78-17 du
6 janvier 1978 (the "**Loi Informatique et Libertés**"), GP is the data controller for the
personal data described in this Policy, except where GP acts as a processor on behalf of an
institutional Customer under a Data Processing Addendum. GP has not appointed a statutory Data
Protection Officer, as its processing does not meet the large-scale or systematic-monitoring
thresholds under Article 37 of the GDPR. Questions and requests concerning this Policy should be
sent to GP's privacy contact at
[privacy@graphical-playground.com](mailto:privacy@graphical-playground.com).

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

2.1. **Account and Profile Data**. When you register for the Services, GP collects your name,
email address, password (stored in hashed form by GP's authentication provider), country, and any
profile information you choose to add, such as an avatar, biography, or links to your work.

2.2. **Customer Content**. Code, shaders, scene graphs, and other materials you submit to be
compiled or rendered on GP's infrastructure ("**Customer Content**," as defined in the Terms of
Service) are processed to provide the Services. Customer Content is not analyzed for its
substance except as necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Services, and is not used
to train machine learning models, consistent with Section 2.5 of the Terms of Service.

2.3. **Payment Data**. If you purchase a paid subscription, certification attempt, or cloud
computing credits, GP's payment processor collects your payment card or bank details directly.
GP does not store full card numbers and receives only transaction identifiers, billing status,
and the billing information needed to issue invoices.

2.4. **Usage Data**. GP collects technical logs, metadata, telemetry, and other usage information
generated by your use of the Services ("**Usage Data**," as defined in Section 2.7 of the Terms
of Service), including IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, features used, and
course progress.

2.5. **Community and Support Data**. If you post on GP Community, the forum, or Discord, GP
collects the content of your posts and messages, which may be visible to other users. If you
contact GP for support, GP collects the content of your request and any attachments you provide.

2.6. **Cookies and Similar Technologies**. GP and its service providers use cookies and similar
technologies on the Services. The categories of cookies used, their purposes, and how to manage
your preferences are described in the
[Cookie Policy](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/cookies), which forms part of this Policy.

2.7. **Special Category Data**. GP does not intentionally collect special category data within
the meaning of Article 9 of the GDPR (such as data revealing racial or ethnic origin, health, or
religious beliefs) and asks that you do not include such data in your profile, Customer Content,
or community posts.

## 3. How GP Collects Personal Data

3.1. GP collects personal data: (a) directly from you, when you register, make a purchase,
submit Customer Content, or contact GP; (b) automatically, through cookies, server logs, and
similar technologies as you use the Services; and (c) from third parties, such as an
authentication provider (for example, when you sign in using a third-party account) or GP's
payment processor.

## 4. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

4.1. GP processes personal data for the following purposes and relies on the corresponding legal
bases under Article 6 of the GDPR:

(a) to create and administer your account, deliver the Services, and process payments, on the
basis that processing is necessary for the performance of a contract with you;

(b) to secure the Services, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce the Terms of Service, on the
basis of GP's legitimate interest in protecting the Services and its users;

(c) to send you service-related communications, such as billing notices and security alerts, on
the basis that processing is necessary for the performance of a contract with you;

(d) to send you optional marketing communications about new courses or features, on the basis of
your consent, which you may withdraw at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any such
communication or your account settings;

(e) to comply with GP's legal obligations, such as retaining invoices for tax purposes, on the
basis of legal obligation; and

(f) to analyze aggregated, de-identified Usage Data to improve the Services, on the basis of
GP's legitimate interest in understanding how the Services are used.

4.2. Where GP relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness
of processing carried out before withdrawal.

## 5. Sharing of Personal Data

5.1. **Infrastructure Providers**. GP shares personal data with the Infrastructure Providers
identified in Section 1.3 of the Terms of Service, including Cloudflare, IONOS, Vercel, Supabase,
and Clerk, who process personal data as processors on GP's behalf to host and operate the
Services. A current list of sub-processors is maintained at
[graphical-playground.com/legal/subprocessors](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/subprocessors).

5.2. **Payment Processor**. GP shares the billing information necessary to complete a transaction
with its third-party payment processor, which processes that information as an independent
controller for payment processing, fraud prevention, and compliance purposes.

5.3. **Legal and Safety Disclosures**. GP may disclose personal data where required to comply
with applicable law, a court order, or a valid legal process, or where GP reasonably believes
disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of GP, its users, or the
public.

5.4. **Business Transfers**. If GP is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or
substantially all of its assets, personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction,
subject to the terms of this Policy and applicable law.

5.5. **No Sale of Personal Data**. GP does not sell personal data.

## 6. International Data Transfers

6.1. Personal data may be processed and stored on servers operated by Infrastructure Providers
located outside the European Economic Area ("**EEA**"). Where such transfers occur, GP relies on
appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European
Commission, consistent with Section 1.3 of the Terms of Service. You may request a copy of the
relevant safeguard by contacting
[privacy@graphical-playground.com](mailto:privacy@graphical-playground.com).

## 7. Data Retention

7.1. GP retains account data for as long as your account remains active and, after account
deletion or termination, for the periods described below.

7.2. Upon termination of your account, GP makes Customer Content available to you for retrieval
for thirty (30) days, after which GP deletes stored Customer Content, consistent with Section
8.2(b) of the Terms of Service.

7.3. GP retains billing records and invoices for ten (10) years following the end of the
applicable subscription term, as required under Article L. 123-22 of the French Commercial Code.

7.4. GP retains Usage Data in de-identified and aggregated form for as long as it remains useful
for the purposes described in Section 4.1(f), and retains identifiable Usage Data for no longer
than is necessary for the security and operational purposes for which it was collected.

7.5. Where you exercise your right to erasure under Section 8.3, GP will delete or de-identify
your personal data except to the extent GP is required to retain it to comply with a legal
obligation, resolve a dispute, or enforce the Terms of Service.

## 8. Your Rights

8.1. If you are located in the EEA, the United Kingdom, or France, you have the following rights
under the GDPR and the Loi Informatique et Libertés with respect to your personal data:

8.2. **Access and Rectification**. The right to obtain confirmation of whether GP processes your
personal data, to access that data, and to have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.

8.3. **Erasure**. The right to request deletion of your personal data, subject to the exceptions
described in Section 7.5.

8.4. **Restriction**. The right to request that GP restrict the processing of your personal data
in certain circumstances, such as while GP verifies the accuracy of contested data.

8.5. **Objection**. The right to object to processing carried out on the basis of GP's legitimate
interest, including profiling, and to object at any time to processing for direct marketing
purposes.

8.6. **Data Portability**. The right to receive personal data you have provided to GP in a
structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, and to have it transmitted to another
controller where technically feasible.

8.7. **Withdrawal of Consent**. Where processing is based on consent, the right to withdraw that
consent at any time, as described in Section 4.2.

8.8. **Exercising Your Rights**. To exercise any of these rights, contact GP at
[privacy@graphical-playground.com](mailto:privacy@graphical-playground.com). GP will respond
within one (1) month of receiving a verifiable request, which GP may extend by a further two (2)
months for complex or numerous requests, in accordance with Article 12(3) of the GDPR. GP may
request additional information to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8.9. **Right to Lodge a Complaint**. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the French
supervisory authority, the Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés (CNIL), at
[www.cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr), or with the supervisory authority of your habitual residence
or place of alleged infringement, without prejudice to any other administrative or judicial
remedy.

## 9. Automated Decision-Making

9.1. GP does not subject you to decisions based solely on automated processing, including
profiling, that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you.
Certification evaluations described in Section 6 of the
[Refund Policy](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/refunds) and the applicable
Product-Specific Terms are reviewed by human evaluators.

## 10. Children's Privacy

10.1. Consistent with Section 2.1 of the Terms of Service, the Services are intended for users
who are at least 18 years of age, or at least 15 years of age with verifiable parental or
guardian consent for the processing of personal data, in accordance with Article 8 of the GDPR
and Article 7-1 of the Loi Informatique et Libertés. GP does not knowingly collect personal data
from a person under 15 without such consent. If GP becomes aware that it has collected personal
data from a person under 15 without the required consent, GP will delete that data.

## 11. Security

11.1. GP implements physical, technical, and administrative security measures designed to protect
personal data against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure, consistent with Section 1.2 of the
Terms of Service. GP's vulnerability reporting process and breach notification commitments are
described in the
[Security Disclosure Policy](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/security-disclosure).

## 12. Third-Party Links and Non-GP Resources

12.1. The Services may contain links to third-party websites or Non-GP Resources, as defined in
Section 1.4 of the Terms of Service. This Policy does not apply to, and GP is not responsible
for, the privacy practices of those third parties. GP encourages you to review the privacy policy
of any third-party site or resource you visit or use.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

13.1. GP may update this Policy from time to time. GP will post the most current version at
[graphical-playground.com/legal/privacy](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/privacy) and will
provide reasonable advance notice of any material change via the GP Platform, on GP's website,
and/or by email to the address associated with your account, consistent with Section 9.1 of the
Terms of Service.
