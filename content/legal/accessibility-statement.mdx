# Accessibility Statement

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Graphical Playground ("**GP**" or "**Graphical Playground**") is committed to making its websites
and the GP Platform (together, the "**Services**") accessible to the widest possible range of
learners, including learners with disabilities. This Accessibility Statement (this "**Statement**")
describes GP's conformance target, known limitations, and how to report an accessibility barrier.

## 1. Conformance Target

1.1. GP's target is conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, version 2.1, Level
AA ("**WCAG 2.1 AA**"), published by the World Wide Web Consortium, and with the technical
requirements of EN 301 549, the European standard for accessibility requirements for ICT products
and services.

1.2. GP is a student project association and does not currently meet the revenue or headcount
thresholds that trigger mandatory accessibility obligations under Article 47 of French Law No.
2005-102 of 11 February 2005 or under Directive (EU) 2019/882 (the European Accessibility Act).
GP nonetheless voluntarily adopts the RGAA (Référentiel Général d'Amélioration de l'Accessibilité),
version 4.1, as its assessment methodology, because it is the reference framework used across
French public and private digital services.

## 2. Conformance Status

2.1. Based on GP's internal, self-conducted assessment, the Services are **partially conformant**
with WCAG 2.1 AA. Partially conformant means that some parts of the content do not yet fully
conform to the standard. GP has not commissioned an independent third-party audit of the Services
as of the date of this Statement.

## 3. Known Limitations

3.1. GP is aware of the following limitations, which it is working to address:

(a) the interactive 3D and shader preview components used to visualize rendering techniques rely
on visual output and do not currently provide an equivalent non-visual alternative;

(b) some code editor components inherited from third-party libraries have incomplete keyboard
navigation and screen reader support;

(c) some archived blog posts and diagrams published before this Statement's effective date may be
missing alternative text; and

(d) captions are not yet available for all video content.

3.2. If you encounter a barrier not listed in Section 3.1, please report it using the process in
Section 5.

## 4. Technical Specifications and Compatibility

4.1. The Services are designed to be compatible with recent versions of major browsers (Chrome,
Firefox, Safari, and Edge) and with commonly used assistive technologies, including screen
readers such as NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver, and with keyboard-only navigation. Accessibility of
the Services depends on the underlying browser and assistive technology functioning correctly;
GP does not control the accessibility features of Non-GP Resources, as defined in the
[Terms of Service](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/terms-of-service).

## 5. Feedback and Contact

5.1. If you cannot access content or a feature on the Services, or want to report an
accessibility barrier, contact GP at
[accessibility@graphical-playground.com](mailto:accessibility@graphical-playground.com) with a
description of the issue, the page or feature affected, and the assistive technology or browser
you were using, if applicable.

5.2. GP will acknowledge your report within five (5) business days and aims to provide a
substantive response, including a plan or timeline for remediation where applicable, within
thirty (30) days.

## 6. Enforcement

6.1. If you are not satisfied with GP's response to an accessibility report, you may refer the
matter to the Défenseur des droits, the French ombudsman with jurisdiction over accessibility
complaints, at [defenseurdesdroits.fr](https://www.defenseurdesdroits.fr).

## 7. Preparation of This Statement

7.1. This Statement was prepared on January 1, 2026, based on a self-assessment conducted by GP
against WCAG 2.1 AA success criteria. GP reviews and updates this Statement at least once a year
and whenever a material change is made to the Services.

## 8. Changes to This Statement

8.1. GP may update this Statement from time to time. GP will post the most current version at
[graphical-playground.com/legal/accessibility](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/accessibility).
