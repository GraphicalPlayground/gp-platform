# Content Moderation Policy

Effective Date: July 26, 2026

This Content Moderation Policy (this "**Policy**") describes how Graphical Playground ("**GP**"
or "**Graphical Playground**") moderates user-generated content on GP Community, the forum,
project submissions visible to other users, and any other part of the GP Platform where you can
post, comment on, or share content (together, the "**User Content**" and, together with the
websites and the GP Platform, the "**Services**"). This Policy operationalizes the
[Acceptable Use Policy](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/acceptable-use) and applies in
addition to it. Capitalized terms not defined in this Policy have the meaning given to them in
the [Terms of Service](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/terms-of-service).

GP provides this Policy in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (the "**Digital Services
Act**" or "**DSA**") and Article 6 of French Law No. 2004-575 of 21 June 2004 for confidence in
the digital economy (the "**LCEN**"). GP qualifies as a micro or small enterprise within the
meaning of Article 19 of the DSA and is accordingly not subject to the obligations set out in
Articles 20, 21, 22, and 24 of the DSA. GP nonetheless provides an internal complaint and appeal
mechanism as a matter of good practice, as described in Section 6 of this Policy.

## 1. Content Standards

1.1. You may not post, upload, or share User Content that:

(a) is illegal under applicable law, including content that infringes a third party's
intellectual property rights, defames a person, or constitutes unlawful hate speech, harassment,
or incitement to violence;

(b) depicts or sexualizes minors in any way, which GP reports to the relevant authority,
including the Internet Watch Foundation or the Plateforme d'Harmonisation, d'Analyse, de
Recoupement et d'Orientation des Signalements (PHAROS), and removes immediately upon detection or
notice, without prior warning to the poster;

(c) contains malware, exploit code intended to compromise another user's device or account, or
instructions for circumventing GP's authentication or billing systems;

(d) shares an answer key, solution file, or other material intended to let another learner pass a
certification assessment without doing the underlying work, which undermines the integrity of
GP's certification described in the Refund Policy;

(e) impersonates GP, a GP contributor, or another user, or misrepresents your affiliation with
GP;

(f) constitutes spam, including repeated unsolicited promotional posts or automated bulk
messaging; or

(g) violates the Acceptable Use Policy.

1.2. Content that is merely critical of GP, the engine, the curriculum, or another user's work is
not prohibited by this Policy. Good-faith critique, bug reports, and technical disagreement are
welcome on GP Community.

## 2. Notice and Action

2.1. **Reporting Content**. You may report User Content you believe violates this Policy using
the report function next to the content, or by emailing
[moderation@graphical-playground.com](mailto:moderation@graphical-playground.com). A report
should identify the content by URL or location, explain why you believe it is illegal or violates
this Policy, and include your contact details, in accordance with Article 16 of the DSA.

2.2. **Copyright Reports**. Reports alleging copyright or trademark infringement are handled
under the
[Copyright Takedown Policy](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/copyright-takedown) rather
than this Policy.

2.3. **Acting on Reports**. Upon receiving a report, GP will assess it in a timely, diligent,
non-arbitrary, and objective manner, as required by Article 16(6) of the DSA. Content that is
manifestly illegal, such as content described in Section 1.1(b), is removed immediately. Other
reports are reviewed by a human moderator, generally within five (5) business days.

2.4. **Statement of Reasons**. Where GP removes, restricts, or demonetizes User Content, or
suspends or terminates an account because of it, GP will provide the affected user with a
statement of reasons in accordance with Article 17 of the DSA, including the factual and legal
basis for the decision, the type of measure taken, and how to appeal it under Section 6 of this
Policy. GP is not required to provide a statement of reasons where the content is deceptive
high-volume commercial content or where a legal obligation prohibits GP from notifying the user,
consistent with Article 17(3) of the DSA.

## 3. Proactive Moderation

3.1. GP may use automated filtering, keyword detection, and human review to identify content that
likely violates this Policy before it is reported, particularly for the content described in
Section 1.1(b) and (c). Automated detection does not replace human review before an account is
suspended or terminated, except where content is manifestly illegal.

## 4. Enforcement Actions

4.1. Depending on the severity and recurrence of a violation, GP may take one or more of the
following actions: (a) remove or restrict visibility of the content; (b) issue a warning to the
account; (c) temporarily suspend posting privileges or the account; (d) permanently terminate the
account, consistent with Section 8.2 of the Terms of Service; or (e) report the content to law
enforcement or another competent authority where required or appropriate.

4.2. GP applies these actions consistently based on the nature and severity of the violation
rather than on the identity of the person posting the content.

## 5. Repeat Infringers

5.1. GP tracks confirmed violations of this Policy at the account level. An account that
accumulates repeated, confirmed violations, or misuses the reporting mechanism in Section 2.1 by
submitting manifestly unfounded reports, will be suspended or terminated, in accordance with
Article 23 of the DSA.

## 6. Appeals

6.1. If GP removes your content or takes action against your account under this Policy, you may
appeal by emailing [moderation@graphical-playground.com](mailto:moderation@graphical-playground.com)
within thirty (30) days of the decision, explaining why you believe the decision was incorrect. A
moderator who was not involved in the original decision will review the appeal and respond within
fourteen (14) days.

6.2. If your appeal is unsuccessful, you retain the right to pursue any judicial remedy available
under applicable law, including before the competent courts of Lyon, France, in accordance with
Section 9.11 of the Terms of Service.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

7.1. GP may update this Policy from time to time. GP will post the most current version at
[graphical-playground.com/legal/content-moderation-policy](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/content-moderation-policy)
and will provide reasonable advance notice of any material change, consistent with Section 9.1 of
the Terms of Service.
