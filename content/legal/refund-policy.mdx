# Refund Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

This Refund Policy (this "**Policy**") describes when Graphical Playground ("**GP**" or
"**Graphical Playground**") will issue a refund, credit, or cancellation for amounts paid through
the GP Platform, including
Starter and Professional subscription fees, cloud computing credits, and project-based
certification fees (collectively, "**Paid Services**"). This Policy is incorporated into, and
forms part of, the
[Terms of Service](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/terms-of-service) and, where
applicable, the
[SaaS Subscription Agreement](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/subscription-agreement).
Capitalized terms not defined in this Policy have the meaning given to them in the Terms of
Service. In the event of any conflict between this Policy and the Terms of Service with respect
to refunds, this Policy controls.

This Policy applies to purchases made by "**Customer**" ("you"), whether a consumer or a business
entity, and does not limit any statutory right of withdrawal, refund, replacement, or price
reduction available to Customer under French or European Union consumer protection law, including
the French Consumer Code (Code de la consommation). Nothing in this Policy waives, restricts, or
requires Customer to give up such statutory rights, and any provision purporting to do so is void
to that extent.

## 1. General Principle

1.1. **Fees Are Generally Non-Refundable**. EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN THIS POLICY, IN THE
TERMS OF SERVICE, OR AS REQUIRED BY APPLICABLE LAW, ALL FEES PAID FOR PAID SERVICES ARE
NON-REFUNDABLE AND ALL PARTIALLY OR FULLY CONSUMED SUBSCRIPTION TERMS, CLOUD COMPUTING CREDITS,
AND CERTIFICATION ATTEMPTS ARE NON-CREDITABLE. GP provisions cloud rendering infrastructure and
reserves evaluator capacity in advance of each subscription term, and prices Paid Services on that
basis.

1.2. **Currency of This Policy**. GP may update the specific refund windows and mechanics
described in this Policy from time to time in accordance with Section 15. The version of this
Policy in effect on the date of your purchase governs that purchase, unless a subsequently
published version expands your rights, in which case the more favorable version applies to any
refund request made after its publication.

## 2. Statutory Right of Withdrawal (EU and French Consumers)

2.1. **14-Day Withdrawal Period**. If you are a consumer resident in the European Union or
France, you have the right to withdraw from a distance contract with GP within fourteen (14)
days of its conclusion, without giving any reason and without penalty, in accordance with
Articles L. 221-18 et seq. of the French Consumer Code. To exercise this right, you must notify
GP before the expiry of the withdrawal period by contacting
[legal@graphical-playground.com](mailto:legal@graphical-playground.com) or by using your GP
account settings. Where you exercise this right validly and in time, GP will reimburse all
payments received from you, including standard delivery or processing fees where applicable,
without undue delay and in any event within fourteen (14) days from the day GP is informed of
your decision to withdraw, using the same means of payment you used for the initial transaction,
unless you expressly agree otherwise and provided you incur no fees from such reimbursement.

2.2. **Exception for Digital Content and Services Already Performed**. In accordance with Article
L. 221-28, 1° and 13° of the French Consumer Code, the right of withdrawal does not apply, or
ceases to apply, once: (a) the supply of digital content not provided on a tangible medium has
begun with your prior express consent and your acknowledgment that you thereby lose your right
of withdrawal; or (b) the Services have been fully performed before the end of the withdrawal
period, with your prior express consent, and your acknowledgment that you will lose your right of
withdrawal once GP has fully performed the contract. GP obtains this consent and acknowledgment
at checkout when you elect to access a Paid Service immediately rather than wait for the
withdrawal period to lapse. Where you have consumed only part of the Services before withdrawing,
GP will refund the fees corresponding to the unused portion only, calculated on a pro rata basis
as described in Section 2.3.

2.3. **Partial Use Before Withdrawal**. If you request withdrawal after having used part of a
Paid Service (for example, having consumed cloud rendering credits or accessed part of a paid
course module) but before the withdrawal period has expired, GP will refund the fees paid less an
amount proportionate to what has been supplied up to the time you informed GP of your withdrawal,
calculated in comparison with the full coverage of the contract.

## 3. Subscription Fees

3.1. **Recurring Subscriptions**. Starter and Professional plan fees are billed in advance for
each monthly or annual subscription term, as described in Section 3 of the Terms of Service.
Except as provided in Section 2 (statutory withdrawal), Section 4 (GP-initiated refunds), or
Section 5 (service failures) of this Policy, fees paid for a subscription term already begun are
non-refundable, including where Customer chooses to cancel, downgrades mid-term, or does not use
the Services during the subscription term.

3.2. **Cancellation Does Not Trigger a Refund**. Cancelling auto-renewal, as described in Section
3.3 of the Terms of Service, stops future charges but does not entitle you to a refund of fees
already paid for the then-current subscription term. Your access continues until the end of the
paid term.

3.3. **Free Trials**. If GP offers a free trial of a Professional plan, no charge will be made
until the trial ends, provided you cancel before the trial expires. If you do not cancel and a
paid subscription begins, standard refund rules under this Policy apply to that subscription;
however, GP may in its discretion offer a full refund of the first billing cycle following a free
trial if requested within seven (7) days of the first charge.

3.4. **Seat and Quantity Changes**. Seats added mid-term are billed as described in Section 3.1
of the Terms of Service and are non-refundable once added. Seats or quantities cannot be
decreased, and no partial refund is issued, for the remainder of a then-current subscription term,
except as required by applicable consumer law.

## 4. Refunds Initiated by GP

4.1. **Termination Without Cause**. If GP terminates your subscription for reasons other than
your breach of the Terms of Service, GP will issue a pro rata refund of pre-paid, unused fees
for the terminated portion of the then-current subscription term, in accordance with Section
8.2(a) of the Terms of Service.

4.2. **Discontinuation of a Service**. If GP discontinues a Paid Service you are subscribed to
during your subscription term and does not migrate you to a substantially similar service, GP
will provide a pro rata refund of fees prepaid for the remaining period of your subscription, in
accordance with Section 9.2 of the Terms of Service.

4.3. **Pricing Changes You Object To**. If GP changes pricing applicable to your existing
recurring subscription and you terminate the affected subscription within thirty (30) days of the
modification notice, GP will refund any pre-paid fees for the terminated portion of the
then-current subscription term, in accordance with Section 3.6 of the Terms of Service.

## 5. Service Failures and Billing Errors

5.1. **Extended Service Unavailability**. If the GP Platform's cloud rendering infrastructure is
unavailable to you for reasons within GP's control for more than seventy-two (72) consecutive
hours during a paid subscription term, and GP is unable to restore access or provide a reasonable
workaround, you may request a credit or pro rata refund for the affected period by contacting
[support@graphical-playground.com](mailto:support@graphical-playground.com) within thirty (30)
days of the outage. This Section 5.1 does not apply to unavailability caused by Force Majeure
(Section 9.4 of the Terms of Service), scheduled maintenance disclosed in advance, or issues
attributable to Non-GP Resources or Customer's own equipment or network.

5.2. **Billing Errors and Duplicate Charges**. If you are charged in error, charged more than
once for the same subscription term or purchase, or charged after a valid and timely
cancellation, GP will refund the erroneous or duplicate amount in full upon verification, without
regard to the general non-refundable rule in Section 1.1.

5.3. **Unauthorized Charges**. If you believe a charge was made without your authorization,
contact GP immediately at
[billing@graphical-playground.com](mailto:billing@graphical-playground.com). GP will investigate
and, where the charge is confirmed unauthorized, refund it and take reasonable steps to secure
your account.

## 6. Certification Fees

6.1. **Certification Attempt Fees**. Fees paid for a project-based certification attempt cover
the review of your submitted project by GP or its designated evaluators and are non-refundable
once an evaluator has been assigned to your submission or your submission has been reviewed,
except as required by Section 2 or Section 5 of this Policy.

6.2. **Withdrawal Before Review Begins**. If you withdraw a certification submission before an
evaluator has begun reviewing it, GP will refund the certification fee in full.

6.3. **Failed Attempts**. A certification attempt that does not meet the required proficiency
standard is not eligible for a refund of the attempt fee. Product-Specific Terms applicable to
the certification program, available at
[graphical-playground.com/product-specific-terms](https://graphical-playground.com/product-specific-terms),
govern re-attempt fees and any applicable discounts.

## 7. Cloud Computing Credits

7.1. **Credits Are Non-Refundable**. Cloud computing credits included with a Professional plan or
purchased separately are non-refundable and have no cash value, except where required by
applicable law or expressly stated by GP at the time of issuance.

7.2. **Expiration**. Credits expire one (1) year after issuance unless otherwise stated by GP at
the time of issuance, consistent with Section 3.5 of the Terms of Service. Expired credits are
not refundable or restorable.

7.3. **Consumed Credits**. Credits already consumed to run compilation, rendering, or compute
jobs on GP's infrastructure are not refundable under any circumstance, as the underlying
infrastructure cost has already been incurred by GP.

## 8. How to Request a Refund

8.1. **Submitting a Request**. To request a refund, contact GP at
[billing@graphical-playground.com](mailto:billing@graphical-playground.com) with: (a) the email
address associated with your GP account; (b) the invoice or transaction reference; (c) the basis
for your request under this Policy; and (d) any supporting information (for example, screenshots
of a service outage or billing error).

8.2. **Review Timeline**. GP will acknowledge receipt of a refund request within five (5) business
days and will provide a decision within fourteen (14) calendar days of receiving all information
reasonably necessary to evaluate the request.

8.3. **Approved Refunds**. Where a refund is approved, GP will process it to the original payment
method within fourteen (14) calendar days of approval, using its third-party payment processor.
Processing times to reflect the refund in your account or statement depend on your bank or card
issuer and are outside GP's control.

## 9. Taxes

9.1. Where VAT (Taxe sur la valeur ajoutée) or other indirect tax was collected on a refunded
amount, GP will refund the tax-inclusive amount actually paid, unless applicable tax law requires
a different treatment.

## 10. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

10.1. **Contact GP First**. If you have a billing concern, GP asks that you contact
[billing@graphical-playground.com](mailto:billing@graphical-playground.com) before initiating a
chargeback or payment dispute with your bank or card issuer, so that GP has the opportunity to
resolve the issue directly.

10.2. **Effect of a Chargeback**. GP may suspend or terminate your access to the Services while a
chargeback or payment dispute is under investigation. If a chargeback is found to be unwarranted
(for example, where the underlying charge was authorized and the Services were delivered as
described), GP reserves the right to recover the disputed amount and any chargeback fees GP
incurs, and to decline future service to the account, without prejudice to your statutory
consumer rights.

## 11. Fraud, Abuse, and Ineligible Requests

11.1. GP reserves the right to deny a refund request, or to deny future refund requests from an
account, where GP reasonably determines that the request is fraudulent, abusive, made in bad
faith, or part of a pattern of repeated purchase-and-refund activity inconsistent with genuine
use of the Services. A denial under this Section 11.1 will state the reason and does not affect
any non-waivable statutory right you may have.

## 12. Currency and Cross-Border Purchases

12.1. Refunds are issued in the currency in which the original charge was made. Where currency
conversion applied to your original purchase, any difference resulting from exchange rate
fluctuation between the purchase and refund dates is not GP's responsibility and is not added to
or deducted from the refunded amount.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

13.1. GP may update this Policy from time to time. GP will post the most current version at
[graphical-playground.com/legal/refunds](https://graphical-playground.com/legal/refunds) and will
provide reasonable advance notice of any material change that reduces your rights under this
Policy, consistent with Section 9.1 of the Terms of Service. Changes do not apply retroactively to
a refund request submitted, or a purchase made, before the change took effect, unless the change
is more favorable to you.

## 14. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

14.1. This Policy is governed by the laws of France, consistent with Section 9.10 of the Terms of
Service. If you are a consumer, you have the right to use the European online dispute resolution
platform at [https://ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr](https://ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr) and the
right to refer a dispute concerning a refund to a certified mediator in accordance with Articles
L. 611-1 et seq. of the French Consumer Code, before pursuing court proceedings.

14.2. If a refund dispute cannot be resolved amicably or through mediation, the sole jurisdiction
and venue is the competent courts of Lyon, France, unless mandatory consumer protection law
grants you the right to bring proceedings before the courts of your place of residence.
